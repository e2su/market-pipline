// How it works
  1. Live data, straight to your browser. The page connects to Binance's public market-data feeds: every trade, the live candle and 24-hour stats arrive as they happen. There's no server in between and no account or key needed.
  2. Indicators are calculated on the spot from up to 500 candles of the timeframe you pick: EMA 20/50/200 for trend, RSI 14 for momentum, MACD 12/26/9 for shifts in momentum, Bollinger Bands (20, 2) and ATR 14 for volatility.
  3. The order book is refreshed every 5 seconds. It adds up the buy and sell orders within 1% of the price, and finds the largest single orders within 2%.
  4. Order flow comes from each trade's aggressor: a market buy hitting the asks counts as buying, a market sell hitting the bids as selling. Trades above the whale threshold are listed.
  5. Derivatives and sentiment come from Binance futures (funding rate, open interest, long/short ratio) and the alternative.me Fear & Greed index. If your region blocks them, those factors are left out of the score.
  6. The market read scores each factor from −1 (bearish) to +1 (bullish), weights them, and scales the total to −100…+100. Trend and timeframe alignment weigh the most. Every factor and its reasoning is in the signal breakdown.
  7. Levels are swing highs and lows (a candle higher or lower than the 3 on each side). The calculator sizes a position so that hitting your stop loses exactly your chosen risk.
Not financial advice. This page shows market data and common technical indicators for education and research. It doesn't know the future, and crypto is highly volatile: you can lose money quickly, especially with leverage. Do your own research and never risk money you can't afford to lose.
